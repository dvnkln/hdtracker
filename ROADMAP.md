# Roadmap

What is planned for hdtracker on the way to version 1.0.0 – in no fixed order and without dates. Have an idea or miss something? Open an [issue](https://github.com/dvnkln/hdtracker/issues).

What is already done: [Changelog](CHANGELOG.md).

## 🎯 Planned for v1.0.0

- [x] **Export your library** – download your own data as a file (Settings → Data).
- [x] **Notifications** – get a push message on your phone or computer when a new season or episode airs or a planned title is released (Settings → Notifications; no extra app needed).
- [ ] **Notifications when dates change** – a message when a release you are waiting for is postponed or moved forward.
- [ ] **Notifications via Pushover** – the same messages through [Pushover](https://pushover.net), for devices that cannot receive push messages from the app.
- [ ] **Streaming overview** – everything you plan to watch, grouped by streaming service: tap “Netflix” and see what is available there.
- [x] **Episode titles for anime** – show episode titles and descriptions for anime where a matching TMDB entry is known.
- [ ] **Multiple users** – an admin manages accounts; every user has their own library and personal settings.
